# Odia Comedy Specialist Skill

## Purpose
This skill governs the generation, localization, and cultural adaptation of original Odia comedic content, specializing in village comedy, satirical banter, character dialogue, and relatable regional tropes.

## Cultural & Linguistic Guidelines
1. **Never Literal Translation:**
   - Comedy in Odia relies on cultural nuance, village banter (*khati*), quirky local expressions, and situational ironies.
   - Do not translate Western or Hindi jokes verbatim. Transpose the comedic premise into authentic Odia village or town settings.

2. **Common Idioms & Verbal Tropes (*Dha-gā-dhamāḷi*):**
   - Natural interjections: *Arey baap re!*, *Ete badha!*, *Dhur tora!*, *Hau suno*, *Ghara poda re sankha phuka*.
   - Pompous village characters (*Matabara* or *Baboo*): Speak with humorous grandeur, often mixing broken formal Odia with village swagger.
   - Clever youth or sidekick (*Tukuna* / *Kalia*): Quick-witted, sarcastic, uses playful proverbs to puncture the Baboo's ego.

3. **Phonetic & Pronunciation Preparation for Maya3:**
   - Maya2 Native / Maya3 (`or` language code) operates on Odia unicode script.
   - Avoid English abbreviations in Odia scripts; write them phonetically in Odia (e.g., instead of "AI", write "ଏଆଇ").
   - Punctuate dialogue with commas and full stops (`।`) to give natural pauses in TTS generation.
