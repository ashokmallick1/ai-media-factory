# Kids Content Director Skill

## Purpose
Governs the creation, structure, lyrics, and child-safety protocols for preschool and early elementary media (ages 2–8).

## Production Sequence
1. **Learning Objective Definition:** Clear concept (e.g., counting 1-10, primary colors, kindness, morning routines).
2. **Lyric & Rhythm Generation:** Simple rhyming scheme (AABB or ABAB), repetitive catchy refrains, clear rhythmic meter suitable for 100–120 BPM.
3. **Pacing & Visual Guidance:**
   - Visual stimulation: avoid frantic jump cuts or over-stimulating flashing lights.
   - Contrast and clarity: bright, primary color palettes, large friendly character silhouettes.
4. **Safety & Suitability Gate:**
   - Zero scary imagery, sudden loud noises, aggressive behavior, or inappropriate slang.
   - Compliance with COPPA and global child online privacy and safety standards.
